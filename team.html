Shawwal Nadeem Siddique

Founder | Managing Partner

S. N. Siddique is an Advocate of the High Court and the Founder of Equilaw & Co.

His practice is focused on delivering clear, strategic, commercially sound, and solutions-oriented legal counsel across a broad range of areas, including corporate and commercial law, litigation, dispute resolution, regulatory compliance, constitutional and criminal law, and recovery proceedings.

He advises individuals, businesses, financial institutions, and corporate clients, with particular emphasis on protecting their interests, managing legal and commercial risks, and developing effective strategies for complex and high-stakes matters.

Through Equilaw & Co., he also advises on cross-border legal matters involving Pakistan, the United Kingdom, the Republic of Ireland, the United States, and Canada, providing clients with practical and strategic legal guidance in matters involving multiple jurisdictions.

His professional approach is grounded in sound legal judgement, meticulous preparation, and effective advocacy, with a clear focus on achieving practical, commercially viable, and meaningful outcomes for every client.
