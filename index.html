About Us

At Equilaw & Co., we provide strategic legal solutions with integrity, precision, and professionalism.

Based in Lahore, Pakistan, we advise individuals, businesses, and corporate clients across a broad range of legal disciplines.

Founded by Mr Shawwal N. Siddique, Advocate High Court (AHC), the firm combines legal expertise with a practical, results-driven approach, delivering tailored advice that protects our clients' interests and achieves effective outcomes.

In addition to our practice in Pakistan, we assist clients with cross-border legal matters involving the United Kingdom, Ireland, the United States, and Canada, including international commercial transactions, regulatory compliance, overseas documentation, and legal coordination across jurisdictions.

Built on trust, discretion, and excellence, Equilaw & Co. is committed to providing clear, commercially focused legal counsel and outstanding client service to both domestic and international clients.
