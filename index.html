About Us

At Equilaw & Co., we deliver strategic legal solutions with integrity, precision, and professionalism.

We combine commercially focused legal advice with robust dispute resolution and advocacy, ensuring our clients receive practical, effective representation at every stage of a legal matter.

Based in Lahore, Pakistan, we advise and represent individuals, businesses, financial institutions, and corporate clients across a broad spectrum of legal disciplines.

Our practice encompasses corporate and commercial advisory, civil, criminal and commercial litigation, constitutional matters, employment law, regulatory compliance, recovery matters, and alternative dispute resolution.

Founded by Mr. Shawwal N. Siddique, Advocate High Court (AHC), the firm combines sound legal expertise with strategic thinking and a results-driven approach. Whether negotiating complex commercial transactions, advising on regulatory obligations, or representing clients before courts and tribunals, we are committed to protecting our clients' interests and achieving favourable outcomes.

In addition to our practice in Pakistan, we assist clients with cross-border legal matters involving the United Kingdom, the Republic of Ireland, the United States, and Canada.

Our international services include commercial transactions, cross-border dispute coordination, regulatory compliance, debt collection, county court representation and advice, overseas documentation, legal opinions, and multi-jurisdictional legal support.

Built upon the principles of trust, discretion, excellence, and unwavering client commitment, Equilaw & Co. is dedicated to delivering clear legal advice, effective advocacy, and commercially sound solutions to domestic and international clients alike.
